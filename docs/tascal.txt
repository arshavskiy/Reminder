Do not copy the whole CRM
Taskal’s pitch is office automation: calendar, billing, WhatsApp, sub-inspectors. Our edge is ingesting existing PDFs with OCR/AI and Gmail. Keep that, then close the office-workflow gaps inspectors feel every day.

How Taskal is packaged
Office CRM
Inspections, invoices, calendar, sub-inspectors, and collection in one place. Modular: pay only for modules used.

Help sections: ניהול תסקירים, כספים, לקוחות, מכונות הרמה, יומן דיגיטלי, תסקירי שטח.

Field + legal
Mobile app that renews a report in ~15 seconds. Templates claimed to match Ministry of Labor rules. Privacy Amendment 13 called out on the site.

Types named: מכונות הרמה, אביזרים, מעליות, מיכלי לחץ, עגורני צריח.

Suggested sequence
1. WhatsApp/SMS on existing reminders. 2. Multi-recipient send of the PDF. 3. Calendar + renew/clone. 4. Bill the client from the inspection. 5. Field app depth. 6. Statutory templates and defects Excel.
